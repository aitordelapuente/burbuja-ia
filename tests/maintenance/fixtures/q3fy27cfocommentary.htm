CFO Commentary on Third Quarter Fiscal 2027 Results

Q3 Fiscal Year 2027 Summary

Revenue for the third quarter was a record $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago and up 18% sequentially. Data Center revenue was a record, driven by demand for our accelerated computing platforms.

During the quarter, we entered into a lease guarantee for a data center campus in Ohio leased to OpenAI for 20 years, under which we may be required to pay up to a maximum of $105 billion over the term of the lease.

Outlook

Fourth-quarter fiscal 2027 outlook: Revenue is expected to be $130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%. GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins are expected to be in line with the prior quarter.

“Neocloud agreements” are described in our Form 10-Q.
